================================================================================
R2 CORS — required for LARGE photo uploads on your live site
================================================================================

The app uploads small images through your Next.js server. Bigger files go
straight from the browser to R2 using a presigned URL. The browser will
block that unless your R2 bucket allows your shop’s origin in CORS.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 — Know your real site origin(s)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

An "origin" is ONLY: scheme + host + port (no path, no trailing slash).

  Good:   https://ishaka-market.vercel.app
  Good:   https://www.yourdomain.com
  Bad:    https://ishaka-market.vercel.app/sell
  Bad:    ishaka-market.vercel.app   (missing https://)

If you use BOTH Vercel and a custom domain, list BOTH in AllowedOrigins.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 — Edit r2/cors.json in this folder
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Open:  r2/cors.json

2. Replace this line with YOUR production origin:
      "https://YOUR-VERCEL-APP.vercel.app"
   Example:
      "https://ishaka-second-hand.vercel.app"

3. Optional: add a comma after that line and another string for a custom domain:
      "https://www.yourdomain.com"

4. Keep localhost lines if you still test uploads locally; remove them if you want.

5. Save the file. Valid JSON: no trailing comma after the last origin string.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 — Apply the policy in Cloudflare (this is what makes it work online)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Log in:  https://dash.cloudflare.com

2. Open:  R2  →  object storage

3. Click the bucket name that matches your env var  R2_BUCKET_NAME

4. Open the  Settings  tab for that bucket

5. Scroll to  CORS policy  (or "Cross-Origin Resource Sharing")

6. Paste the ENTIRE contents of  r2/cors.json  (the [ ... ] JSON array)

7. Save

Cloudflare may take a short time to apply changes. Try a large upload again
after 1–2 minutes.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4 — Confirm it matches your deployment
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- The bucket you edited must be the same one as  R2_BUCKET_NAME  on Vercel
  (or your host).

- The origin you added must match exactly how users open the shop (including
  https and www vs non-www).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Troubleshooting
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Large uploads fail, small ones work  →  CORS missing or wrong origin, or
  policy not saved on the correct bucket.

- "Failed to fetch" on upload  →  Often CORS or wrong R2 public URL; also
  check  R2_PUBLIC_BASE_URL  in server env.
